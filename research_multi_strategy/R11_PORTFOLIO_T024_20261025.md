# T024 / R11 组合化 short straddle

- **基础研究：** T023 R11 BTC/ETH DVOL + Binance 1h synthetic short ATM straddle
- **研究身份：** historical portfolio simulation only；不部署、不接真实下单
- **execution_evidence：** `unknown`（期权价格、盘口、成交与结算均为合成）
- **requested data window：** 2021-01-01 00:00:00 UTC through 2026-10-01 00:00:00 UTC exclusive
- **effective position window：** Deribit public DVOL endpoint in this run has no observations before 2022-01-01; 2021 rows remain unavailable/unknown and are not imputed. Binance 1h prices cover 2021 so any later DVOL extension can be replayed.
- **entry-IV correction：** T023 candidates must use the Deribit DVOL 1h close at the exact UTC entry timestamp. The prior portfolio result used a daily DVOL close and is invalidated; this rerun consumes only the corrected T023 positions artifact.

## Portfolio rules

1. Initial equity is 50,000 USDT. At most one new short ATM straddle is opened per UTC day. The candidate stream is the daily T023 BTC/ETH stream; when both are available, choose the asset with fewer currently open positions, breaking ties by BTC. This is a deterministic capacity rule and is not a predictive selection rule.
2. Each selected position has a 30-day holding period and a 2,500 USDT fixed entry notional in the fixed-size run. At most 30 positions can be open concurrently.
3. Initial margin is modeled as 30% of entry notional and maintenance margin as 15%. Margin is reserved for open positions but is not treated as a PnL cost. If equity is below the aggregate maintenance line, record a `margin_call`, stop opening new positions, and force-close active positions at the synthetic pro-rata mark PnL available from the T023 terminal ledger. The pro-rata mark is a margin-only approximation because T023 stores terminal PnL rather than a full option mark surface.
4. The risk-budget run sizes every new position at 2% of current equity, with no more than 30 active positions. PnL, option premium, hedge turnover and margin scale linearly from the 2,500 USDT T023 ledger.
5. Net PnL scenarios use option premium spread assumptions `{0.2%, 0.5%, 1.0%, 2.0%}` and perp hedge fees `{8bp, 16bp}`. Baseline is 0.2% + 8bp.

## Portfolio outputs

For every sizing/scenario combination report the daily equity curve, annualized return, maximum drawdown as a percentage of prior equity peak, annualized daily Sharpe, monthly CVaR95/CVaR99, worst month, margin calls, forced liquidations, and terminal equity. `bankruptcy_probability` is estimated both as the deterministic historical-path indicator and by 1,000 block-resampled daily-PnL paths (30-day blocks); it is a historical stress statistic, not a calibrated real-world probability.

The fixed-size and risk-budget runs are compared under identical candidate selection and cost scenarios. The forward-paper numeric gate is `annualized_return > 10%` and `max_drawdown_pct > -30%`; a numeric pass remains blocked when `execution_evidence=unknown`.
