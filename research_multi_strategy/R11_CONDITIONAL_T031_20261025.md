# T031 / R11 trailing-DVOL conditional short straddle

- **研究身份：** preregistered historical synthetic rule plus paper-only forward gate
- **execution_evidence：** `unknown` for historical simulation; forward remains `forward_paper` and never sends an order
- **asset：** BTC primary; ETH is an optional comparison run
- **entry IV：** Deribit DVOL 1h close at the exact UTC 00:00 entry timestamp
- **condition：** enter only when `entry_iv >= median(previous 252 valid daily entry IV observations)`
- **absolute IV threshold：** none
- **holding period：** 30 days
- **historical selection：** non-overlapping positions; the next entry cannot precede the prior expiry
- **executable scale:** 0.1 BTC per option leg for forward paper; historical PnL is scaled from the T023 2,500 USDT per-leg ledger to 0.1 BTC per-leg notional
- **cost scenarios:** synthetic comparison at 0.2% premium spread + 8 bp hedge fee, and primary executable-cost scenario at 2.25% premium spread + 16 bp hedge fee

## No-lookahead rule

The 252-observation median is computed from the 252 valid entry IV observations strictly before the current entry timestamp. The current IV is never included in its own threshold. If fewer than 252 prior observations exist, the decision is `skipped_warmup`. A current IV below the trailing median is recorded as `skipped_low_iv` and creates no position.

The input IV is the corrected T023 `entry_iv_source=deribit_dvol_1h_close_at_entry`. Daily DVOL closes are not used. Any result produced before the T023 lookahead repair is invalidated.

## OOS protocol

The 2022–2023 period is the reference/training period for describing the rule. The 2024–2026 period is the OOS test period. The primary test is fixed at the median condition; threshold multipliers around the median are descriptive sensitivity checks only and do not select the rule.

Report non-overlapping `n`, mean bps, 30-day block bootstrap CI/p, effective independent sample, MDE, status, annual splits, and unconditional short-straddle comparison. MDE above 12 bp remains `not_estimable` and cannot be called significant profitability.

## Forward paper behavior

The conditional paper runner records the live entry IV, trailing median, and decision. On `skipped_low_iv`, it writes a decision/ledger record and does not create a fill or position. The unconditional baseline remains available through the same stateful runner with the condition disabled. Both paths remain paper-only; no order or hedge endpoint is connected.
